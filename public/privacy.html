Your privacy

The short version: we keep as little as we can, we never ask for your real name, and you can delete everything whenever you like.

What we keep

  • The display name you choose (please don’t use your real name).
  • Your reading: book titles, minutes and when you logged them.
  • Your star ratings, bingo squares, bonus minutes and any prizes you win.
  • Your tag code, stored scrambled so nobody can read it back.

What we don’t keep

Your real name, email, address, phone number, library card number, school or location. There are no ads and no tracking.

Who can see what

  • Other readers see your display name and total minutes on the leaderboard. Book titles and ratings appear in suggestions and Community favourites without saying who read them.
  • Library staff can see display names, minutes and reading entries, so they can run the challenge, give bonus minutes and prizes, and remove anything unkind.

Where it’s kept, and for how long

Everything is stored securely with our database provider, Supabase. Reading from this summer is cleared when the next season starts. Deleted accounts are erased 14 days after deletion. Text on these pages uses fonts from Google Fonts.

Your choices

  • Delete your account any time: there’s a button at the bottom of your page. Your name comes off the leaderboard and your tag stops working straight away. Everything is erased for good 14 days later. That gap means a library desk can undo it if it was a mistake, or if someone else used your tag.
  • Made a mistake? You can remove reading you logged in the last 7 days.
  • Questions, or want something changed? Ask at any library desk, or contact Kāpiti Coast District Libraries.

Parents and caregivers: you’re welcome to ask us what’s stored for your child, or to have it deleted straight away.
